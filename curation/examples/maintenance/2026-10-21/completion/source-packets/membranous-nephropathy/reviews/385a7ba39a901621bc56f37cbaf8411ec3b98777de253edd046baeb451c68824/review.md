## Summary to evaluate

Primary membranous nephropathy shares an antigen-specific podocyte autoantibody principle, but antigen-defined subtypes differ in frequency, serology and clinical associations and are increasingly treated as partly distinct entities. Autoantibodies, IgG4-predominant though not IgG4-exclusive, bind a podocyte-associated antigen, not all of which are cell-surface proteins: PLA2R in most inspected adult cases, THSD7A in a smaller PLA2R-negative subgroup, NELL-1 in a further share of PLA2R-negative cases that varies widely by cohort, and semaphorin 3B, HTRA1 or rarer putative targets beyond those. The evidence differs in kind by antigen: deposit-eluted IgG recognized PLA2R and THSD7A, patient sera recognized NELL-1, semaphorin 3B and HTRA1, and the seven putative mass-spectrometry targets were recovered from immune complexes and co-localized with IgG in the deposits without comparable serological evidence. Anti-PLA2R levels tracked clinical status and proteinuria, and epitope spreading beyond the cysteine-rich domain was an independent risk factor for poor renal outcome. HLA-DQA1 and PLA2R1 risk loci were associated with biopsy-proven disease in white-ancestry cohorts.

Antigen-specific rodent models supply directional evidence: podocyte expression of human PLA2R1 produced antibodies, deposits and nephrotic syndrome, lymphocyte-deficient animals developed neither, and Thsd7a-knockout mice resisted antibody transfer. These do not establish one human effector route. Patient-biopsy assays detected all three complement initiation pathways, with proximity assays favouring the classical pathway; factor-B-null mice were protected and lectin-pathway blockade preserved slit proteins in culture. A newly added retrospective comparison of 17 PLA2R-associated and nine NELL1-associated cases found lower C3 and C4d deposition in NELL1 disease. Deposition is not functional activation, proteomic complement differences were not significant, and this study did not test slit-diaphragm injury. Effector evidence also differs by system: C5b-9 was required for slit-protein proteolysis in culture and C5 deficiency partly reduced albuminuria, while membrane-attack-complex inhibition did not restore albumin permselectivity on a glomerulus-on-a-chip and anti-THSD7A antibodies injured podocytes in C3-deficient mice. No initiating pathway is established as universal. The under-1-to-4.9% frequencies concern seven putative targets, not NELL-1; malignancy-associated NELL-1 disease falls outside this primary-only scope. The primary-secondary boundary is a curation choice. Evidence rests on agent-inspected abstracts; therapy efficacy and independent domain review remain outside the completed work.
