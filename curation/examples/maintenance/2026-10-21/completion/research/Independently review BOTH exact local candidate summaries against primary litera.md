Primary membranous nephropathy shares an antigen-specific podocyte autoantibody principle, but antigen-defined subtypes differ in frequency, serology and clinical associations and are increasingly treated as partly distinct entities. Autoantibodies, IgG4-predominant though not IgG4-exclusive, bind a podocyte-associated antigen, not all of which are cell-surface proteins: PLA2R in most inspected adult cases, THSD7A in a smaller PLA2R-negative subgroup, NELL-1 in a further share of PLA2R-negative cases that varies widely by cohort, and semaphorin 3B, HTRA1 or rarer putative targets beyond those. The evidence differs in kind by antigen: deposit-eluted IgG recognized PLA2R and THSD7A, patient sera recognized NELL-1, semaphorin 3B and HTRA1, and the seven putative mass-spectrometry targets were recovered from immune complexes and co-localized with IgG in the deposits without comparable serological evidence. Anti-PLA2R levels tracked clinical status and proteinuria, and epitope spreading beyond the cysteine-rich domain was an independent risk factor for poor renal outcome. HLA-DQA1 and PLA2R1 risk loci were associated with biopsy-proven disease in white-ancestry cohorts.

Antigen-specific rodent models supply directional evidence: podocyte expression of human PLA2R1 produced antibodies, deposits and nephrotic syndrome, lymphocyte-deficient animals developed neither, and Thsd7a-knockout mice resisted antibody transfer. These do not establish one human effector route. Patient-biopsy assays detected all three complement initiation pathways, with proximity assays favouring the classical pathway; factor-B-null mice were protected and lectin-pathway blockade preserved slit proteins in culture. A newly added retrospective comparison of 17 PLA2R-associated and nine NELL1-associated cases found lower C3 and C4d deposition in NELL1 disease. Deposition is not functional activation, proteomic complement differences were not significant, and this study did not test slit-diaphragm injury. Effector evidence also differs by system: C5b-9 was required for slit-protein proteolysis in culture and C5 deficiency partly reduced albuminuria, while membrane-attack-complex inhibition did not restore albumin permselectivity on a glomerulus-on-a-chip and anti-THSD7A antibodies injured podocytes in C3-deficient mice. No initiating pathway is established as universal. The under-1-to-4.9% frequencies concern seven putative targets, not NELL-1; malignancy-associated NELL-1 disease falls outside this primary-only scope. The primary-secondary boundary is a curation choice. Evidence rests on agent-inspected abstracts; therapy efficacy and independent domain review remain outside the completed work.

Primary minimal change disease (MCD) is represented here as an immune-associated podocytopathy with heterogeneous upstream pathogenesis, foot-process effacement and nephrotic proteinuria. Anti-nephrin autoantibodies identify a subset: one multicentre cohort detected them in 44% of adults with MCD and 69% with active untreated disease. Levels tracked activity and proteinuria, fell during remission and reappeared before relapse; prevalence varies by assay and cohort. Human biopsy findings associate antibodies with podocyte IgG. Nephrin immunization in mice induced an MCD-like syndrome, nephrin phosphorylation and podocyte cytoskeletal changes. Patient IgG transferred to a rabbit caused proteinuria, effacement without deposits and nephrin phosphorylation. These experiments support a pathogenic antibody route in selected systems, without establishing each intracellular step or explaining antibody-negative disease. Anti-nephrin-positive patients also had IgG4 predominance, Th2-skewed cytokines and a non-significant plasmablast trend; their sequence is unresolved. A newly added human study found higher hemopexin protease activity despite lower hemopexin concentration during MCD relapse, compared with remission. This supplies a separate observational circulating-factor signal, not proof that hemopexin causes human MCD.

The antibody-transfer experiment used total IgG from one patient in one rabbit, rather than purified nephrin-specific antibody; animal sufficiency does not establish universal human necessity. The hemopexin study compared 18 relapsing patients, 23 patients in remission after prednisolone, other glomerulopathies and controls; treatment and disease-stage confounding remain. Its kidney-tissue activity assay did not establish in vivo permeability-factor causation. Peripheral CD4 activation at onset remains an association. IL-13 overexpression in rats caused albuminuria, foot-process fusion and changes in nephrin and B7-1 expression, supporting a model-specific route without establishing that IL-13 is necessary in humans. Urinary IL-8 rose in relapse, yet that study did not support a disease-specific pathogenic role for IL-8 or TNF-alpha under its tested conditions. No single upstream factor is established as necessary or sufficient across primary MCD. Concurrent steroid-induced remission and antibody clearance do not isolate the mediator of treatment response. Secondary causes, other glomerulopathies and treatment efficacy are excluded from this synthesis. Human observations and animal experiments retain separate graph contexts; most sources, including the newly added hemopexin report, were inspected at abstract level. ## Membranous Nephropathy

**Verdict: needs_revision.**

The strongest supported parts are that MN is an antibody-mediated disease with subepithelial immune complexes, complement-containing deposits, and podocyte injury leading to proteinuria [[1.2]](https://consensus.app/papers/the-classical-pathway-triggers-pathogenic-complement-seifert-zahner/08101c1eee485710807642b1bed24280/) [[9.1]](https://consensus.app/papers/alternative-pathway-dysregulation-and-the-conundrum-of-borza/77602f1761055797bd95415f8f68bcc0/) [[9.2]](https://consensus.app/papers/alternative-pathway-dysregulation-and-the-conundrum-of-borza/77602f1761055797bd95415f8f68bcc0/) [[9.3]](https://consensus.app/papers/alternative-pathway-dysregulation-and-the-conundrum-of-borza/77602f1761055797bd95415f8f68bcc0/). The candidate is also right that antigen-defined entities differ biologically, because NELL1 cases show distinct staining patterns and clinical associations compared with PLA2R-associated disease [[10.1]](https://consensus.app/papers/nell1-is-a-target-antigen-in-malignancyassociated-caza-hassen/a9768ca7688a5549833dbd5aed09d7ce/) [[10.2]](https://consensus.app/papers/nell1-is-a-target-antigen-in-malignancyassociated-caza-hassen/a9768ca7688a5549833dbd5aed09d7ce/) [[5.2]](https://consensus.app/papers/nell1-membranous-nephropathy-clinical-associations-andeen-kung/dd264236b96d54c9b2ebeec38b3487a5/).

Major revision is needed around **NELL1 framing**. Several later papers show that many NELL1 MN cases carry secondary associations such as malignancy, lipoic acid, NSAIDs, autoimmunity, mercury exposure, infection, or GVHD, and one review states these account for the majority of cases in some series [[5.1]](https://consensus.app/papers/nell1-membranous-nephropathy-clinical-associations-andeen-kung/dd264236b96d54c9b2ebeec38b3487a5/) [[5.3]](https://consensus.app/papers/nell1-membranous-nephropathy-clinical-associations-andeen-kung/dd264236b96d54c9b2ebeec38b3487a5/) [[5.4]](https://consensus.app/papers/nell1-membranous-nephropathy-clinical-associations-andeen-kung/dd264236b96d54c9b2ebeec38b3487a5/) [[6.2]](https://consensus.app/papers/characteristics-and-outcomes-of-nell1-membranous-avasare-clark/0a427db927e2531a92e37234791014e3/) [[6.1]](https://consensus.app/papers/characteristics-and-outcomes-of-nell1-membranous-avasare-clark/0a427db927e2531a92e37234791014e3/). Because the candidate includes NELL1 inside a “bounded primary” map, it should explicitly state that this is a **curation decision against a mixed literature**, not merely that malignancy-associated NELL1 is out of scope [[5.1]](https://consensus.app/papers/nell1-membranous-nephropathy-clinical-associations-andeen-kung/dd264236b96d54c9b2ebeec38b3487a5/) [[11.1]](https://consensus.app/papers/clinical-and-pathological-characteristics-of-li-liang/7f19bee613a15f468c4a45ca98347ab1/) [[11.2]](https://consensus.app/papers/clinical-and-pathological-characteristics-of-li-liang/7f19bee613a15f468c4a45ca98347ab1/).

Complement claims also need tighter wording. One strong study found all three pathways in human biopsies but favored **classical pathway** activation by proximity ligation and showed reduced disease in complement-deficient mice and after C3 silencing [[1.3]](https://consensus.app/papers/the-classical-pathway-triggers-pathogenic-complement-seifert-zahner/08101c1eee485710807642b1bed24280/) [[1.1]](https://consensus.app/papers/the-classical-pathway-triggers-pathogenic-complement-seifert-zahner/08101c1eee485710807642b1bed24280/) [[1.4]](https://consensus.app/papers/the-classical-pathway-triggers-pathogenic-complement-seifert-zahner/08101c1eee485710807642b1bed24280/) [[1.5]](https://consensus.app/papers/the-classical-pathway-triggers-pathogenic-complement-seifert-zahner/08101c1eee485710807642b1bed24280/). But other studies support **lectin pathway** involvement in PLA2R-associated disease, including higher MBL, C3, and C4d in active disease and protection of cultured podocytes by lectin-pathway blockade [[2.1]](https://consensus.app/papers/lectin-complement-pathway-activation-is-associated-with-li-zhang/a8481bccef435a8fa11fba7a7f65dee9/) [[2.2]](https://consensus.app/papers/lectin-complement-pathway-activation-is-associated-with-li-zhang/a8481bccef435a8fa11fba7a7f65dee9/) [[12.1]](https://consensus.app/papers/altered-glycosylation-of-igg4-promotes-lectin-complement-haddad-lorenzen/a9c7d69bd3e65e64872dd647fb849374/) [[12.2]](https://consensus.app/papers/altered-glycosylation-of-igg4-promotes-lectin-complement-haddad-lorenzen/a9c7d69bd3e65e64872dd647fb849374/). The newer NELL1-vs-PLA2R abstract supports lower C3/C4d deposition in NELL1 but does **not** establish less functional complement activation, since proteomic differences were not significant [[12.3]](https://consensus.app/papers/altered-glycosylation-of-igg4-promotes-lectin-complement-haddad-lorenzen/a9c7d69bd3e65e64872dd647fb849374/) [[12.4]](https://consensus.app/papers/altered-glycosylation-of-igg4-promotes-lectin-complement-haddad-lorenzen/a9c7d69bd3e65e64872dd647fb849374/). ## Minimal Change Disease

**Verdict: needs_revision.**

The candidate correctly presents primary MCD as **heterogeneous**, with no single upstream mechanism established across all cases [[14.1]](https://consensus.app/papers/antinephrin-autoantibodies-in-posttransplant-recurrent-shirai-hattori/eeba2cac935f5e368e07d54747b0ff5d/) [[14.2]](https://consensus.app/papers/antinephrin-autoantibodies-in-posttransplant-recurrent-shirai-hattori/eeba2cac935f5e368e07d54747b0ff5d/). It is also well supported that anti-nephrin autoantibodies mark a subset, are enriched in active untreated disease, correlate with activity, and decline in remission [[3.2]](https://consensus.app/papers/autoantibodies-targeting-nephrin-in-podocytopathies-hengel-dehde/fcc175416c5b5f038c8845dab4644dd2/) [[3.3]](https://consensus.app/papers/autoantibodies-targeting-nephrin-in-podocytopathies-hengel-dehde/fcc175416c5b5f038c8845dab4644dd2/) [[3.1]](https://consensus.app/papers/autoantibodies-targeting-nephrin-in-podocytopathies-hengel-dehde/fcc175416c5b5f038c8845dab4644dd2/) [[7.2]](https://consensus.app/papers/antinephrin-antibodies-in-adult-chinese-patients-with-shu-huang/52ec93daf84c5bcf9cb0eb4187a0c871/) [[4.2]](https://consensus.app/papers/afucosylated-igg-in-idiopathic-nephrotic-syndrome-spinelli-garbarino/2aaa6d869bba58d493fe87e4c9139130/).

The main problem is **prevalence framing**. The candidate cites the 44% adult MCD figure and 69% active untreated figure from the NEJM cohort, but later studies using other assays found much lower adult MCD positivity, around 19% in one cell-based assay cohort, 18% in another multicenter ELISA cohort, and 15% in a pediatric/young-adult INS cohort, while also detecting antibodies in FSGS [[3.2]](https://consensus.app/papers/autoantibodies-targeting-nephrin-in-podocytopathies-hengel-dehde/fcc175416c5b5f038c8845dab4644dd2/) [[3.3]](https://consensus.app/papers/autoantibodies-targeting-nephrin-in-podocytopathies-hengel-dehde/fcc175416c5b5f038c8845dab4644dd2/) [[15.1]](https://consensus.app/papers/antinephrin-antibody-a-potential-biomarker-of-minimal-chen-chen/3ea75cbcca89598685d73c443ad7d179/) [[8.1]](https://consensus.app/papers/3131-multicenter-study-of-antinephrin-antibodies-in-corona-alonso/22b64865e4a95782a16f6c595208d326/) [[4.1]](https://consensus.app/papers/afucosylated-igg-in-idiopathic-nephrotic-syndrome-spinelli-garbarino/2aaa6d869bba58d493fe87e4c9139130/). The candidate says prevalence varies by assay and cohort, which is good, but it should say more directly that **replication is qualitatively supportive yet quantitatively inconsistent** [[7.3]](https://consensus.app/papers/antinephrin-antibodies-in-adult-chinese-patients-with-shu-huang/52ec93daf84c5bcf9cb0eb4187a0c871/) [[15.2]](https://consensus.app/papers/antinephrin-antibody-a-potential-biomarker-of-minimal-chen-chen/3ea75cbcca89598685d73c443ad7d179/) [[4.3]](https://consensus.app/papers/afucosylated-igg-in-idiopathic-nephrotic-syndrome-spinelli-garbarino/2aaa6d869bba58d493fe87e4c9139130/).

The candidate is appropriately cautious on pathogenicity. Active immunization with nephrin induced nephrotic syndrome, slit-diaphragm IgG localization, nephrin phosphorylation, and cytoskeletal injury in mice [[3.4]](https://consensus.app/papers/autoantibodies-targeting-nephrin-in-podocytopathies-hengel-dehde/fcc175416c5b5f038c8845dab4644dd2/) [[3.5]](https://consensus.app/papers/autoantibodies-targeting-nephrin-in-podocytopathies-hengel-dehde/fcc175416c5b5f038c8845dab4644dd2/). IL-13 overexpression in rats caused albuminuria, hypoalbuminemia, hypercholesterolemia, and up to 80% foot-process fusion with nephrin/podocin downregulation, while later mechanistic work linked this model to Vav1-Rac1 signaling in podocytes [[16.1]](https://consensus.app/papers/overexpression-of-interleukin13-induces-lai-wei/5febb57c2e625d31b99dffbeb1206c09/) [[16.2]](https://consensus.app/papers/overexpression-of-interleukin13-induces-lai-wei/5febb57c2e625d31b99dffbeb1206c09/) [[16.3]](https://consensus.app/papers/overexpression-of-interleukin13-induces-lai-wei/5febb57c2e625d31b99dffbeb1206c09/) [[17.1]](https://consensus.app/papers/novel-role-of-vav1rac1-pathway-in-actin-cytoskeleton-chan-ng/b267aabb47af5781b27ba6331860b602/) [[17.2]](https://consensus.app/papers/novel-role-of-vav1rac1-pathway-in-actin-cytoskeleton-chan-ng/b267aabb47af5781b27ba6331860b602/). But the broader IL-13 story is not one-way: another 2023 experimental paper reported context-dependent effects in which IL-13 alone worsened injury markers yet reversed damage in coculture with M1-conditioned medium, so the candidate should note that **IL-13 evidence is model-dependent rather than uniformly pathogenic** [[18.1]](https://consensus.app/papers/contribution-of-il33ilc2mediated-th2-cytokines-during-liu-liu/5a470fab04ca5005a12a952f0e5ca0cc/) [[18.2]](https://consensus.app/papers/contribution-of-il33ilc2mediated-th2-cytokines-during-liu-liu/5a470fab04ca5005a12a952f0e5ca0cc/).
