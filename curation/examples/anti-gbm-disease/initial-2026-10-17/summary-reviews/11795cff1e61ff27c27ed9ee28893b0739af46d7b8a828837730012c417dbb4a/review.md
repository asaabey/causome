## Summary to evaluate

Patient kidney/lung-bound autoantibodies recognized α3NC1 and α5NC1 monomers in antigen-specificity assays. In biochemical assays, the native cross-linked α345NC1 hexamer concealed antibody-reactive conformations present on α3NC1/α5NC1 monomers.

These selected findings describe a bounded candidate, whose overall explanation has not been independently checked against current literature. Binding specificity does not show equal pathogenic contribution of α3 and α5 antibodies; Alport comparators are excluded as mapped disease. Does not establish the initiating in-vivo trigger or prove that hexamer disruption precedes human disease. In the 15-adult one-arm phase 2a trial, anti-GBM antibody levels were below the assay reference range six hours after imlifidase. Drug targeting and approval do not establish every proposed mechanism or benefit in every phenotype.
