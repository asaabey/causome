## Summary to evaluate

C3 or C4 deficiency reduced proteinuria and capillary thrombosis in heterologous anti-GBM nephritis. Transfer of serum or renal-eluate IgG from three anti-GBM patients produced acute glomerulonephritis in recipient monkeys. Patients with Goodpasture disease displayed clonally expanded α3-specific CD4 T-cell repertoires.

These selected findings describe a bounded candidate, whose overall explanation has not been independently checked against current literature. Dose-sensitive model effect; protection was overcome by increased antibody dose. Passive transfer supports antibody pathogenicity in recipients; it does not explain human autoimmunity initiation. Treatment efficacy has not been synthesized in this summary.
