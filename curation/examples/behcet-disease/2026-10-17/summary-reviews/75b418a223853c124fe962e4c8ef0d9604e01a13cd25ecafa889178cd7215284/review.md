## Summary to evaluate

Behçet disease combines genetic susceptibility with recurrent inflammation affecting mucosa, skin, eyes and, in subsets, vessels, the nervous system, joints or bowel. HLA-B*51 and ERAP1 show a statistical interaction, while IL10 and IL23R–IL12RB2 associations implicate immune regulation without establishing a specific initiating antigen. Human blood and culture studies connect IL-21 with increased Th17 responses and fewer regulatory T cells; IL-23 can increase IL-17 production, and IL-17 and IFN-γ are elevated in active uveitis. A parallel neutrophil branch converges on extracellular traps (NETs): patient neutrophils form NETs, NET markers associate with vascular involvement, and DNase reduces thrombin generation in patient plasma ex vivo. A 2026 exploratory study links CD177-positive neutrophil-derived NETs and histone H3.1 to endothelial senescence and neutrophil recruitment in culture. In the intestinal subset, a separate PBMC–epithelial co-culture study links PLK1-dependent TNF release to IFI16–STING–TBK1 signalling and epithelial apoptosis. These findings connect selected immune and tissue-injury processes; phenotype links to aneurysms, neurologic injury and visual morbidity remain clinical observations.

The overview combines different patient subsets and experimental systems; it does not establish one causal circuit operating in every patient. Genetic cohorts overlap, most reports were inspected at abstract level, and the newer endothelial and intestinal mechanisms need independent replication. IFN-γ inhibited IL-17 production in one culture assay despite both cytokines being elevated in active uveitis. Subcutaneous secukinumab did not meet the primary endpoint in the Behçet uveitis SHIELD trial, qualifying the inference that IL-17 elevation predicts benefit from blockade. Apremilast reduced oral ulcers in a 207-person placebo-controlled trial and has a verified US adult oral-ulcer indication; that trial excluded major organ involvement and does not establish vascular or neurologic protection. In a separate 52-person severe vascular/CNS trial, infliximab produced more complete responses at week 22 than cyclophosphamide with glucocorticoids in both arms; this is indication-specific evidence, and Behçet is absent from the inspected US infliximab label. Microbial triggers, broader immune pathways and treatment options remain incompletely curated. Historical cohort outcomes are not current-treated risk estimates. Independent scientific review and appraisal calibration remain pending.
