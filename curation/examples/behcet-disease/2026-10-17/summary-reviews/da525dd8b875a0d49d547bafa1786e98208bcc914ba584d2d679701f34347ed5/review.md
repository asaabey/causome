## Summary to evaluate

Circulating NET markers were higher in active than inactive disease and healthy donors. A selected vascular cohort included 23 Behçet patients with nonpulmonary aneurysms. Lesional IL-17A expression was associated with extensive perivascular recruitment of NET-forming neutrophils.

These selected findings describe a bounded candidate, whose overall explanation has not been independently checked against current literature. Abstract-level inspection; selected study population or assay, not proof of a universal disease mechanism. Selected clinical cohort; no population prevalence or molecular cause established. In the 207-person phase 3 trial, apremilast reduced oral-ulcer burden over 12 weeks versus placebo; major-organ involvement was excluded. Drug targeting and approval do not establish every proposed mechanism or benefit in every phenotype.
