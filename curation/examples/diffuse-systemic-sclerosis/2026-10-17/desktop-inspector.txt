THERAPEUTIC INTERVENTION

APPROVED · SMALL-MOLECULE THERAPY

Nintedanib

Inhibits receptor tyrosine kinases including PDGFR, FGFR and VEGFR; mapped fibroblast effects are indirect.

INDICATION & STATUS

US: slowing pulmonary-function decline in adults with SSc-ILD. No skin indication asserted.

United States approvals where explicitly verified; EULAR guidance separately labelled · Completed clinical study; population and endpoints restricted as described
Last verified: 2026-10-17 · Editorial: candidate
Indirect: Lung fibroblasts
Intervention limits
Diarrhoea was frequent; not a fibrosis cure or proven skin treatment.
