## Summary to evaluate

This bounded Sjögren map connects epithelial and immune activation around type I interferon and BAFF. In human-cell experiments, patient-serum autoantibodies to RNA-binding proteins and dying-cell material generated an RNA-containing immune-complex stimulus for plasmacytoid dendritic cells. Interferon-producing cells and interferon-responsive gene expression were observed in affected glands, while interferons induced epithelial BAFF secretion in culture. BAFF regulates B-cell survival; glandular B-cell aggregates can organise into ectopic germinal centres associated with local anti-Ro/SSA and anti-La/SSB production in a subset of biopsies. Together these findings suggest amplification between immune cells and epithelium, rather than proving a complete causal loop in every patient. A complementary human tissue study found selective seromucous acinar-cell loss alongside GZMK-positive CD8 T-cell accumulation; functional assays linked GZMK to epithelial interferon signalling. Salivary dysfunction and clinical dryness are represented, but direct lacrimal mechanisms remain incompletely curated.

Important limits remain: BAFF secretion after interferon stimulation did not significantly differ between patient and control cultures, BAFF/GC associations were not uniformly positive, and human AQP5 localisation studies conflicted. BAFF-overexpressing and early pDC-depletion mouse experiments support mechanisms in those systems, not sufficiency or reversibility in established human disease. Ianalumab met its phase 2b model-based dose-response objective, although the individual 300 mg comparison crossed the null. Dazodalibep improved phase 2 systemic activity and a symptom composite in populations partly including associated RA/SLE, limiting primary-only inference. Later sponsor phase III updates are development evidence, not complete peer-reviewed efficacy appraisals or approval. TRACTISS found no significant rituximab benefit for its primary fatigue/oral-dryness endpoint despite a secondary salivary-flow improvement. The selected therapies remain experimental for the mapped Sjögren indication; no treatment recommendation follows from targeting these pathways. Most sources were inspected at abstract level, study independence is incompletely established, and independent scientific review and score calibration remain pending.
