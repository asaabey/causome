# Manual scientific review

The illustrative fixture tests rendering and provenance mechanics. It does not establish disease mechanisms, treatment efficacy, regulatory approval, or evidence confidence. Fixture documentation is not a primary scientific citation.

For Milestone 2, curate one bounded SLE circuit from directly verified sources:

- Preserve named biological entities, direction, polarity, phenotype, cell/tissue context, and disease stage.
- Separate association from causal interpretation, and initiation from maintenance.
- Attach each scientific assertion to the precise evidence item and source location it supports.
- Record qualifying and contradictory findings where identified. A missing evidence category means none recorded, not proof none exists.
- Distinguish primary study identity from multiple reports or reused cohorts.
- Verify therapy targets, status, jurisdiction and indication, and verification date independently.
- Use permitted summaries and excerpts; do not redistribute restricted full text.
- Record reviewer identity, decision, limitations, and date. Abstain when the source does not support the exact interpretation.

Source concepts in IDEA.md and proto-sle.md guide candidate selection only. They do not replace evidence verification. Public scientific review follows publication-approval.md.
